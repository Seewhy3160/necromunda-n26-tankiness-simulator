Necromunda (N26) Tankiness Simulator

How many hits it takes to put one fighter Down, against a fixed pool of common weapons, and what each credit of defensive gear buys. No dice are rolled.

Unofficial fan-made tool. Necromunda is a trademark of Games Workshop Ltd.
Works offline — no network, no storage, no accounts. Sibling of the N26 Attack Simulator, whose engine it reuses.
